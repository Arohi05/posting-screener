# 2-minute demo script (read it in your own words)

**0:00 - 0:20 The problem.**
"Looking for internships, I check every posting by hand and ask seniors on WhatsApp whether a company is genuine. Some risky postings look completely normal."

**0:20 - 0:50 Risky example.**
Open the page. The Risky example is loaded. "This one is unpaid, promises an 8 lakh job later, and the company claims thousands of people hired. It says Risky and shows exactly which rules fired. It doesn't ask for any fee, which is the part I got wrong first."

**0:50 - 1:15 Good fit and Check carefully.**
Click Good fit. "A QA internship in Bengaluru that matches my skills." Click Check carefully. "This one is in Gurugram, so it says my location doesn't match."
Paste a real posting you saw today and press Screen.

**1:15 - 1:40 How well it works.**
Scroll to the numbers. "I tested it on 29 real postings I labelled by hand. It scores 79% against 52% for always guessing the middle answer, and catches 8 of the 9 risky ones. But I wrote the rules after reading those same postings, so the real score is probably lower."

**1:40 - 2:00 What's next.**
"Next I want to test it on postings it hasn't seen and add a way for users to report wrong verdicts."

Keep it under 2 minutes. Record on your own screen, with your own voice.
