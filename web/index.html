    It caught 8 of the 9 postings labelled Risky. None of those 9 asked for a fee. They were unpaid roles promising large salaries later, mass-hiring claims and "comment INTERESTED" posts.

    Read this number with care: the rules were written after reading these same 29 postings, so 79% is likely too high. A fresh set of postings is the fair test.

    What it does not do

    It does not collect postings from LinkedIn, Internshala or Naukri, because their terms forbid scraping. You paste the text. It cannot check whether a company really exists.

    What to do with a verdict

    Good fit means no red flags and your skills and city match. Check carefully means something is missing or off. Risky means two or more red flags, or a fee. Never pay to get an internship, whatever the label says.
